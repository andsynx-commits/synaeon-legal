Synaeon - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Synaeon is a private, single-user app. Its owner built it for his own small business, and he is its only user. It is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks customers.

What it reads

When the owner connects his own QuickBooks Online company, Synaeon reads customers, invoices, payments, items and expenses from that company. It reads only. It never creates, changes, sends or deletes anything in QuickBooks, and it never moves money.

Where the data goes

The data Synaeon reads is stored on the owner's own computer, in his private database. Sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's protected credential store.

When the owner asks Synaeon a question about his books, the figures needed for that answer are sent to the AI service that writes Synaeon's reply, for that answer only. Apart from that, the data is not shared with anyone. It is never sold, and it is not used for advertising.

Disconnecting

The owner can disconnect at any time, from QuickBooks (Apps, then Disconnect) or inside Synaeon. When he disconnects, Synaeon stops reading. Copied data can be removed from his computer whenever he chooses.

Changes

Any change to this policy is posted on this page.
